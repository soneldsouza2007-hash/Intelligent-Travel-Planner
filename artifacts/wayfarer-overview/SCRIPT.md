# Wayfarer — Travel, Considered

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Change the destination and Wayfarer finds places and reshapes the plan around that city; budget estimates stay clear about what is and is not verified.
- **Audience:** People planning trips, including groups who want a shared view of the plan and spend.
- **Format and runtime:** 16:9 landscape, 29 seconds.
- **Identity:** Use Wayfarer's existing dark evergreen `#24443d`, coral `#dc8368`, warm cream, Fraunces display serif, DM Sans, and DM Mono. The existing wordmark is lowercase `wayfarer`, paired with the map icon already used in the app. No external logo asset is available or needed.
- **Hero:** The city-specific itinerary itself: map pin, discovered place names, route, budget estimate.
- **Motion:** Map-pin scale-throughs, route lines that become UI dividers and budget meters, and masked split/reveal transitions.
- **Arc:** A fast destination hook expands into real Singapore place results, turns those places into a legible plan, then slows for the budget treatment and a quiet brand resolution.
- **Payoff:** The route pin and itinerary resolve into the app's map mark and wordmark; the outgoing coral pin becomes the opening pin on the loop.
- **Sound:** One instrumental bed with a restrained, upbeat travel-documentary pulse; no voiceover or sound effects. Beat alignment remains unverified until playback is checked.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–5.50s / 5.50s — “NOT JUST A PIN.”

**Purpose:** Establish immediately that this is a destination-aware travel planner, not a generic map.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A dark evergreen field carries a fine, low-contrast street-grid pattern. On the left, oversized cream type owns the frame; on the right, a coral map pin floats over a simplified street network. The pin is the magnifying aperture: its center reveals a warm-cream map beneath the dark field. The map stays graphic and schematic, not a claim to exact cartography.

**Exact copy and holds:** `NOT JUST A PIN.` begins at 0.12s and remains through 1.70s. `SINGAPORE` resolves at 1.75s and remains through the cut. `LOCAL PLACES. ONE CITY.` appears at 3.05s and holds through 4.35s. No narration.

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.30s: coral pin snaps to scale 1 from 0.72 with a stiff spring; a small route dot appears alongside it within 0.2s. 0.18–0.62s: `NOT JUST A PIN.` reveals in two masked word groups. 0.65–1.25s: camera pushes toward the pin while the pin aperture reveals the cream map. 1.00–1.55s: street lines draw outward from the pin; three quiet neighborhood dots answer in sequence. 1.75–2.25s: `SINGAPORE` replaces the hook with a short tracking compression. 2.30–3.10s: coral route strokes join the dots; one stop marker pops at each junction. 3.05–4.35s: `LOCAL PLACES. ONE CITY.` unmasks beside the map. 4.35–5.50s: the pin accelerates forward and expands to cover the center; the map grid shears into two cream panels as the route remains visible beneath.

**Transition contract:** The center coral pin carries into Shot 02 at the same center point and scale. Its lens becomes the destination-search target while the two cream panels finish unfolding. Use a pin scale-through plus a two-panel split; do not leave a solid-color wipe tail.

**Assets and technique:** CSS street-grid and route SVG, CSS pin/aperture, existing Lucide `Map` icon only for the final app mark, Fraunces/DM Sans/DM Mono. No photo or external map tiles; map geometry is clearly illustrative.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental begins with a light percussive pulse. No beat sync is claimed.

## Pre-code review

- **Variety:** Five major beats differ in framing and mechanism: asymmetric hook, close search interface, wide map-world, instrument-like budget close-up, quiet centered end card.
- **Motion:** Each beat has a substantive subject action after its entrance and begins its outgoing transition in the final 15–20%.
- **Claim safety:** Only verified Singapore result names and planner budget/split behavior are stated. Map geometry is schematic; there are no fabricated venue prices, stop-day assignments, ratings, or live data claims.
- **Readability:** Copy is short, with map names and interface labels in stable zones. The longest venue name receives its own line and full hold.
- **Cohesion:** Existing Wayfarer palette, wordmark spelling, app mark, and font families carry through all five shots.
- **Loop:** The end pin matches the opener's pin position and field, so the film can loop without a blank frame.
